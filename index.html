Privacy & reliability notes

Text messages, photos and voice notes are encrypted in the browser with AES-GCM before broadcast. Photos are resized/compressed in the browser and limited to a small Realtime-safe payload; one photo can be sent at a time. Voice notes are limited to 10 seconds, sent live through Realtime, and are not saved to local history or uploaded to Storage. Only offline pending messages are stored briefly by the server; live messages are not archived.

This remains an unaudited prototype. The hosting page's JavaScript could be changed by a compromised account/site. Anyone with the room code can join. No identity verification or forward secrecy. Supabase receives encrypted payloads and connection metadata. Use only a publishable/anon key in frontend code—never a service-role key. Realtime handles live delivery. Encrypted server queue is used only if the recipient does not acknowledge the live message. Voice notes use encrypted, size-limited Broadcast payloads rather than file uploads, so long recordings are intentionally unsupported.
