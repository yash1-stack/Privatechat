Privacy & reliability notes

Text messages, photos and voice notes are encrypted in the browser with AES-GCM before broadcast. Photos are resized/compressed in the browser and limited to a small Realtime-safe payload; one photo can be sent at a time. Voice notes are limited to 10 seconds, sent live through Realtime, and are not saved to local history or uploaded to Storage. The server does not store a message archive in this design.

This remains an unaudited prototype. The hosting page's JavaScript could be changed by a compromised account/site. Anyone with the room code can join. No identity verification or forward secrecy. Supabase receives encrypted payloads and connection metadata. Use only a publishable/anon key in frontend code—never a service-role key. Realtime is not durable offline messaging; a disconnected recipient may miss a message or voice note. Voice notes use encrypted, size-limited Broadcast payloads rather than file uploads, so long recordings are intentionally unsupported.
